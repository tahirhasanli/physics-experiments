Small balls bounce elastically off the walls and each other — a 2D gas. Drag the piston (the thick bar on the right) to compress or expand the box and watch the pressure respond. Raise the temperature slider and every ball speeds up (and turns redder); lower it and they slow down (and turn bluer). Values are in abstract simulation units, not SI — the point is the relationships between them, which is exactly what the ideal gas law PV = NkT describes.
