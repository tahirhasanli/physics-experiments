Physics Sims

A small set of PhET-style interactive simulations — pure HTML/Canvas, no build step. Open any of these on this device or any other; each one is a link.

Spring-Mass Oscillator

Drag masses onto a spring, tune k/mass/damping, and watch it oscillate. Includes a light-gate photogate and a stopwatch for measuring the period.

Projectile Motion

A catapult launches a projectile with adjustable angle, speed, and air drag. Traces the trajectory and shows velocity components live.

Wave Interference

Two-source wave interference — see constructive and destructive patterns build up in real time.

Fizeau Speed-of-Light Experiment

A recreation of Fizeau's rotating-toothed-wheel setup for measuring the speed of light.

Point Source & Lens Rays

A point or collimated source through a thin lens — adjust focal length and source type to see how the ray fan converges.
