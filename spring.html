Spring-mass oscillator. Starts with nothing hanging on the spring — drag a spare weight from the table up onto the hook, or use the mass slider, to load it. Once loaded, drag the hanging mass up or down to stretch or compress the spring, then release to watch it oscillate. Adjust the spring's natural (rest) length, its stiffness k, and the mass. The spring's own mass is no longer a free variable — it's modeled as a stainless-steel coil whose wire gets thicker (and heavier) as k goes up, just like a real spring set, and that extra mass feeds into the physics as effective mass = mass + spring mass/3. The laser and sensor sit in the small crate on the table — drag either one anywhere on the canvas to set up a light gate; the sensor lights up when it catches the beam. A 60 cm ruler is tucked along the left edge — drag it anywhere to measure.


Spring mass (stainless steel wire, thickens with k): 6.0 g
