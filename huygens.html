Click near the wavefront to plant a secondary source (a "ray"); drag one to move it, select and press Delete to remove it. Each ray grows a small tangent wavelet circle — the curve tangent to all of them is the next wavefront. Click "Advance" to promote it and repeat. Drag the wall to move it, or Ctrl/Cmd+drag one of its ends to rotate it — it blocks rays and the wavefront everywhere except through its slits. This is manual on purpose: leave a slit empty and the wave just stops there (no diffraction); place a few rays yourself right inside a slit and watch the wavefront curl around into the shadow — that curling is the classic Huygens argument for diffraction.
