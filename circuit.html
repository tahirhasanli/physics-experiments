Drag a part from the menu onto the board to place it. Drag from a lead (the small circle at each end) to pull out a wire and connect it to another lead. Drag a part's body to move it; Ctrl/Cmd+drag a lead to rotate it. Click a switch to flip it, or a wire to change which way it bends. Click a part to select it — edit its value or delete it. An LED only lights when current flows through it in the direction of its arrow.
