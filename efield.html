Drag a "+" or "−" charge from the panel onto the board to place it, or drag out the field probe to read the exact field at a point — its screen shows the live reading as you move it. Drag anything already placed to reposition it; click to select it and adjust its strength or delete it. Charges are in whole numbers of elementary charges (e); the board is a real 20cm-tall sheet, so every field reading is a real N/C value from Coulomb's law.
